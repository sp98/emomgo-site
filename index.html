EMOMGo - Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

EMOMGo is a free Android app for EMOM (every minute on the minute) workouts: it runs the interval timer, logs your reps and weight for each round, and shows how your lifts progress over time.

Data Collection

EMOMGo does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. The app has no internet access, no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. All data stays on your device.

The app stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Workouts and sessions you create (exercise names, rounds, interval length, target reps and weight)
  • Workout logs (reps and weight for each round, the date, and the effort rating you choose)
  • Settings (rest time, voice cues, keep screen on, effort logging, optional weekly goal)

Permissions

EMOMGo requests the following permissions, all used locally on-device:

PermissionWhy it's needed
Foreground ServiceTo keep the workout timer running when the screen is off or you switch apps
NotificationsTo show the running timer in the notification shade
Wake LockTo keep the timer accurate while the screen is off

Voice Cues

When voice cues are on, EMOMGo uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine to announce rounds and countdowns. EMOMGo only passes it short phrases such as "Round 3" — nothing about you.

Backups

If Android backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include EMOMGo's data in your device backup (for example, Google One backup). This is handled by Android under your Google account's settings; EMOMGo itself never sends data anywhere.

Third-Party Services

EMOMGo uses no third-party services, SDKs for advertising, analytics, or crash reporting.

Data Sharing

EMOMGo does not share any data with anyone.

Deleting Your Data

You can remove individual log entries from the Progress screen. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of its data from your device.

Children's Privacy

EMOMGo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
